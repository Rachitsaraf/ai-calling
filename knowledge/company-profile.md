
# VOBPL – Company Knowledge Base

## 1. Company Overview

Company Name: Vyankatesh Oxides and Batteries Pvt. Ltd.
Short Name: VOBPL
Website: https://www.vobpl.com
Industry: Battery Recycling and Lead-Based Products
Established: 2018
Formerly Known As: Balaji Industries
Location: Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India

VOBPL is involved in battery recycling, lead refining and
manufacturing lead-based products. The company focuses on
quality, sustainability, innovation and responsible recycling.

## 2. Vision

- Deliver sustainable and high-quality lead products.
- Operate with ethics, transparency and responsibility.
- Protect the environment and employee wellbeing.
- Exceed customer expectations through innovation.

## 3. Mission

- Become Central India's largest lead manufacturer.
- Achieve 100% customer satisfaction with zero harm to the environment.
- Reduce carbon footprint by 50% by 2030.
- Promote sustainable growth through recycling and advanced lead products.
