## Company Overview

- Full name: Vyankatesh Oxides and Batteries Pvt. Ltd.
- Short name: VOBPL
- Website: https://www.vobpl.com
- Industry: Battery recycling, lead refining, and lead-based products.
- Established: 2018, originally as Balaji Industries.
- Location: Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India.

of lead and lead-alloy products. Its stated priorities include product quality,

## Company History

The company website describes this timeline:

- **2018:** Vyankatesh Oxides and Batteries Pvt. Ltd. was founded for
  responsible lead refining and established as Balaji Industries.
- **2020:** Manufacturing expanded to Unit 2 at Bhanwarasala.
- **2022:** In-house quality testing and a Spectro machine were introduced;
  the site says turnover reached "100 Million" but does not state the currency.
- **2023:** The company entered Litharge and Grey Oxide production and added a
  dedicated laboratory.

- **2026:** The site reports establishing Unit 3 at Dhankhedi.

## Products and Website-Published Details

The following summaries and technical figures are published on the product
pages. They are useful for answering general questions, but may be indicative
or change. When quoting a specification, say it is listed on the website and
offer to have the VOBPL team confirm the current datasheet and grade. Never
promise stock, price, or suitability for a customer's process.

### Refined Lead

- High-purity primary lead supplied as ingots.
- The page lists a soft, bluish-grey appearance, density of 11.34 g/cm³,
  purity of at least 99.97% Pb (FAQ: typically 99.97%–99.99%), and melting
  point of 327.5°C.
- Listed uses: lead-acid batteries, radiation shielding, cable sheathing and
  corrosion protection, ammunition, and chemical or pigment production.
- The page mentions plastic-strapped bundles or palletised shipments and
  export-ready packaging.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/refined-lead

### Lead-Antimony Alloy Ingots

- Lead-antimony ingots intended for applications requiring greater hardness,
  strength, and corrosion resistance than pure lead.
- Composition is customisable; the FAQ gives a usual antimony range of 1%–6%,
  depending on application.
- The page lists a silvery, crystalline appearance and melting point of
  327.5°C.
- Listed uses: battery grids and plates, cable sheathing and electrical
  components, ammunition and specialty alloys, and flame-retardant compounds.
- The page mentions plastic-strapped bundles or palletised shipments and
  export-ready packaging.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/lead-antimony-alloy-ingots

### Lead Alloys

- Custom lead alloys may blend refined lead with antimony, calcium, tin, or
  selenium to meet customer requirements.
- The page lists ingots weighing 20–22 kg and customisable composition.
- Listed uses: battery grids, cable sheathing, radiation shielding,
  ammunition, and casting.
- The page mentions plastic-strapped bundles or palletised shipments and
  export-ready packaging.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/lead-alloys

### Litharge (PbO / Lead Monoxide)

- Yellow powder produced by controlled oxidation.
- The page lists density of approximately 9.35 g/cm³, purity of at least 99%
  as PbO, and melting point of approximately 800°C.
- Listed uses: PVC stabilisers and plastic processing, glass and ceramics,
  paints, lubricants, and specialty chemicals.
- The page says it is packed in moisture-proof bags or drums and should be
  stored in a dry, well-ventilated area away from acids and oxidising agents.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/litharge

### Grey Oxide (Lead Sub-Oxide)

- Controlled powder for lead-acid battery plate manufacture; the page says
  particle size and free-lead content are controlled.
- The page lists density of approximately 9.35 g/cm³, purity of at least 99%
  as PbO, and melting point of approximately 800°C. As these figures may not
  fully describe lead sub-oxide, quote them only as website-listed and confirm
  the current datasheet with the company.
- The page describes production by a controlled ball-mill process and lists
  battery plates, industrial pigments, chemical intermediates, and specialty
  lead chemicals as uses.
- The page mentions 25 kg moisture-proof bags, with bulk or palletised
  shipping also available.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/grey-oxide

### Lead-Antimony Wire

- Lead wire alloyed with antimony; the page lists 1%–6% antimony, a diameter
  range of 1–12 mm, 99.97% purity, a smooth and oxide-free finish, and a
  melting range of 250–300°C.
- Listed uses: battery grid wires, ammunition and defence components, cable
  sheathing and shielding, and radiation-protection systems.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/lead-antimony-wire

### Lead Balls

- Precision-manufactured lead balls.
- The page lists IS / ASTM standards, density of 11.34 g/cm³, purity of
  99.9%–99.99%, and a FAQ size range of 2–50 mm; custom dimensions may be
  requested.
- Listed uses: ammunition and defence, ball milling and grinding media,
  counterweights, vibration dampening, radiation shielding, and ballast.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/lead-balls

### Lead Ingots / Bricks

- Refined lead ingots or bricks for industrial use.
- The page lists IS / ASTM standards, density of 11.34 g/cm³, purity of
  99.97%–99.99%, unit weights of 20–40 kg, and a melting point of
  approximately 327°C.
- Listed uses: lead-acid batteries, radiation shielding, ballast weights,
  soundproofing, alloy production, and casting. The page says custom
  dimensions, weights, and packaging may be available.
- Details: https://www.vobpl.com/lead-ingots
